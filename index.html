V20 Hardware & DTB Identification

Board Revision Details

  • PCB Stamp: R36S-V20 2025-05-18
  • SoC: Rockchip RK3326 (Quad-Core Cortex-A35 @ 1.5GHz)
  • RAM: 1GB DDR3L (Verify single vs. dual chip layout)
  • Display: 3.5" IPS 640×480 (4:3)

Panel & DTB Compatibility

  • Always back up the original BOOT partition before flashing.
  • Copy your stock .dtb files (e.g., rk3326-r35s-linux.dtb or rg351mp-kernel.dtb) to a safe folder.
  • If the screen stays black after flashing ArkOS, replace the .dtb file on the new BOOT partition with your backup.

Clone vs. Genuine Check

  • Genuine ArkOS: Boots directly into EmulationStation; volume buttons work natively without EmuELEC overlays.
  • Clone / EmuELEC Units: Often use single RAM chip layouts and require K36 / Clone ArkOS builds.
  • Check the battery compartment label and PCB silkscreen under the battery.

SD Card & Firmware Setup

1. Replace Stock SD Cards

Stock microSD cards fail frequently. Use a reliable brand (SanDisk, Samsung, Kingston) — 16GB/32GB for OS (TF1) and 64GB–256GB for ROMs (TF2).

2. Backup Original BOOT

Insert the stock card into your PC and copy every .dtb, .ini, and boot file before formatting anything.

3. Flash & First Boot

Flash the R36S ArkOS image using Rufus or balenaEtcher. Insert into TF1-OS and wait 3–5 minutes for partition expansion.

Quick Troubleshooting

Black screen on boot (Blue/Red LED is on)
Your display panel requires a different DTB file. Put the TF1 SD card back into your computer, open the BOOT drive, and copy your original backup .dtb files over (or test Panel 1–4 DTB files from the ArkOS DTB folder).
Red blinking LED / Console won't power on
Charge using a standard 5V 1.5A / 2A USB-A to USB-C cable. High-wattage USB-C PD fast chargers (laptop/phone brick chargers) will not negotiate power properly with the R36S charging circuit.
TF2 (Games) SD card not detected
Format your secondary card as exFAT or FAT32, insert it into the TF2 slot, go to Options → Advanced → Switch to SD2 for ROMs in EmulationStation, and let ArkOS generate the folder structure.
